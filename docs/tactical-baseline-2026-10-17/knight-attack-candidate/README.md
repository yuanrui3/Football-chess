# Native-knight attack prototype (not proposed for release)

`engine.mjs`, SHA-256 `80ac412c6cabb1a0a0a9a6aeb27e723aaf0e39f748f40e5868fd8c1236c69036`, starts from complete-v5 and adds Tactical-only values for a knight receiving, carrying once and finishing with its native L-shaped kick. Matching pass ranks gain expected knight finishing potential ×700. The leaf uses the maximum of immediate receiving finishes and that future knight potential ×250, avoiding counting one opportunity twice. Rules, Casual, probability coefficients, search nodes and learned weights are unchanged.

All 28 existing selected AI regressions and four new mirrored route/counterplay checks passed. The natural route has no immediate knight shot at the receiving square, then a legal carry opens a 74.3% keeper-reacted finish. An actual Casual king reply can tackle the knight with its unchanged 50% clean success rate. Actual Tactical defensive positioning can occupy the carrying destination first and reduce the route's exposure by more than 70%. The test therefore establishes a legal, contestable plan rather than assuming a free goal. The previous complete-v5 engine already finds this particular natural pass; the prototype reinforces ranking and future evaluation.

The four paired 240-action dev scenarios versus frozen Casual ended **1 win, 3 draws, 0 losses**, goals **1–0**, shots **18–3**, xG **2.6314–0.3697**. All transitions were valid and every Tactical decision stayed within 1,400 nodes. Matched v4 and v5 prefixes have the same score/WDL, with 21 shots and 3.0736/3.1952 xG. One v6 goal arrives earlier—action169 versus v4's226/v5's203—so its later kickoff reset changes the trajectory and raw xG alone cannot establish weaker play. There is no clear measured goal-volume improvement supporting release of the additional complexity.

`comparison.json` and `../knight-attack-v6/` contain the complete screen. No held-out outcomes were consulted. Further receiving potential should explicitly price both actual defensive positioning actions; static future opportunities can otherwise encourage deferring moderate finishes.

The separate `engine-v7-attack-only.mjs` prototype removes v5's knight-defense rank changes and keeps only this offensive experiment on balanced-v4. The `*-fast.mjs` files add a geometrically exact eligible-knight prefilter. These alternatives were prepared but **not benchmarked or proposed for release**; they remain separate immutable files.
