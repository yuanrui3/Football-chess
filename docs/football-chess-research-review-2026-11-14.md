# Football Chess as a benchmark for planning under uncertainty

**Research status. Closed on November 15, 2026.** The shot experiment met its depth coverage requirement and missed its improvement threshold. The later RL and move-ranking experiments showed no repeatable gain at equal decision time. [Final research closeout](football-chess-rl-closeout-2026-11-15/README.md) · [Shot pilot](shot-probability-pilot-2026-11-14/README.md)

**Original rationale for the pilot.** Football Chess offers a way to test whether an agent can make a good sequence of decisions under an opponent's replies, and whether access to the game's probability model improves learning under a fixed compute budget. The strongest case combines two things. A good finish can require several earlier positioning and possession decisions, and the rules give a checkable probability distribution for the shot after the keeper replies. The pilot was designed to establish the value of that combination before a larger training program or a claim of benchmark novelty.

This review concerns the association-football implementation in `yuanrui3/Football-chess` at `45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403`, the revision used in the 1,600-game study. The repository credits Serrano Saunders's Football Chess as its inspiration. The review compares published game AI benchmarks, closely related tabletop and digital rules, stochastic planning and learning methods, and football action-valuation research. The novelty claim is limited to the research affordances supported by those comparisons. [Reviewed game and attribution](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/README.md) · [Tournament study](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/tree/codex/tactical-search-sensitivity/docs/search-sensitivity-2026-11-13)

## Decision depth in the game

An attacking side can spend separate allowances on dribbling, an off-ball move, and one pawn or king reposition. The defending side can change the board before the kick. A pass can create a new carrier or leave a contested loose ball. On an eligible shot, the defending keeper sees the declared target or the public pair of targets, then moves or holds. The engine samples the shot afterward. A catch, a rebound, a corner, and a goal create different next decisions. The value of an early move can therefore depend on whether a later receiving lane survives a defender's reply, whether the receiver can finish against the keeper, and who gets the ball if the shot fails. [Action budgets and legal moves](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L538) · [Rules and shooting](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/README.md#shooting-model-19) · [Shot resolution](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L1309)

The existing exercises show concrete dependencies. Under the documented model 16 audit, the checked three-preparation defense in two far-post studies reduced the opponent's best immediate finish to **9.30%**, below the **10%** target. The strongest enumerated route with at most two preparations left **13.91%**. In a later exercise audit, a stronger defender attacked after the first delivery in exercise 27. The revised example needed an extra off-ball screen to protect the carrier before the shot. These are bounded examples with specific objectives and reply models. They establish that multi-action dependencies exist and give the pilot positions where shorter plans can be challenged explicitly. [Far-post audit](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/docs/exercise-studies.md#keeper-rush-model-16-compatibility) · [Exercise defense audit](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/docs/exercise-defence-depth-2026-10-31/README.md)

Decision depth should be measured by the minimum meaningful choices needed to meet a tactical objective under a stated reply model. Record attacker decisions, defender decisions, random transitions, and elapsed possession rounds separately. The 240-action game cutoff measures episode length. Completed search depth measures how far a particular algorithm got. Neither quantity tells us how many useful decisions a position demands. Work on the effective horizon makes a related distinction in single-agent environments. Football Chess needs its own adversarial measurement, including checks for shorter alternatives and better opponent replies. [Laidlaw et al., ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/3e18f7152b5e1696e73722dc5121b6f2-Abstract-Conference.html)

## What the scoring model lets researchers test

For a fixed board, legal shot declaration `d`, and legal keeper reaction `k`, `shotModel` gives the conditional goal probability. An ordinary shot has one declared target. For an eligible disguised shot, the keeper sees a pair and commits to **one** reply before the engine draws a target with equal probability. The immediate goal probability is the average for that same reply across the two targets. That information order matters. In a regression fixture, the best defended disguised shot exceeds the best defended ordinary shot among the two eligible endpoints by more than **20 percentage points**, with the underlying finishing multipliers held fixed. This establishes a measurable effect of knowing less about the endpoint before committing. [Public mixture](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L1142) · [Order of the draws](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L1309) · [Disguise fixture](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/tests/disguised-shot.test.mjs#L262)

The same model exposes the trade-off after a save. In the documented aligned-rook example, a legal keeper step forward reduces the shot's goal chance from **19.01% to 17.58%**, while the probability of holding the ball **given a save** falls from **63.36% to 41.00%**. Catching can hand the keeper possession and a distribution choice. A parry can leave a contested rebound or concede a corner. Two reactions ordered by immediate goal prevention can be ordered differently by their total expected defensive value. The fixture demonstrates the trade-off in outcome probabilities. Establishing a reversal of the best decision requires continuation values. Production Casual and Tactical both choose the keeper reply by minimizing immediate goal probability, so the node ladder did not compare keeper policies. [Keeper rush and handling](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/docs/keeper-rush.md) · [Shared keeper policy](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/tests/disguised-shot.test.mjs#L247)

There are two distinct targets for learning. For an **immediate finish**, take the minimum goal probability across all legal keeper replies, including holding. Then take the maximum across eligible declarations if the objective is to choose the strongest available finish. The exercise grader already implements the first calculation. For **longer-horizon action value**, weight each outcome's immediate reward and resulting state value by its probability, and account for subsequent decisions. Averaging the goal roll out gives an exact conditional label for whether that shot scores. It removes the Bernoulli variance of that local label. Full-match variance and action values still depend on how the sampled outcome changes possession, restarts, future shot counts, and tactics. The existing search also approximates long wide-shot landing scatter, so exact scoring probabilities alone cannot certify an exact continuation model. [Exercise grader](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/exercise-session.js#L62) · [Search successors and wide-shot approximation](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L2799) · [Ballard's weighted chance search](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0004370283800150)

The surrounding literature also narrows the claim. Moschini models a sequential striker–keeper contest with stochastic scoring, with the keeper's positioning choice preceding the striker's aim. The MESSI software project already supplies seeded 11-a-side physics simulation and captures transitions for learning and audit. Football Chess's contribution is the combination of **measurable buildup depth and an auditable shot subgame** in a discrete environment. A result strongest on positions where an earlier setup move changes the best defended shot, and where the shot's failure branches matter, would use that combination. [Moschini, *Economics Letters*, 2004](https://www.card.iastate.edu/files/inline-files/Moschini-Economics-Letters-04.pdf) · [MESSI research software](https://github.com/UoS-CIDA-Lab/MESSI)

## What our experiments establish

The fixed-action tournament found a distinct regression when the Tactical setting increased from 1,400 nodes and a depth cap of 8 to 5,600 nodes and a cap of 12. In 400 head-to-head games, mean completed depth rose from **4.211 to 6.507**, while the deeper policy lost **0.152 xG per game** (95% seed-cluster interval **−0.175 to −0.129**) and conceded **383 tackles versus 91**. The comparison changed both cap and budget, and used the same heuristic ranking and evaluator. Search-depth pathology is documented in the literature. The observed result motivates checking shortlist omissions, leaf estimates, and phase-specific effects separately. It cannot distinguish those mechanisms. [Extended ladder report](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/codex/tactical-search-sensitivity/docs/search-sensitivity-2026-11-13/results/ladder-up-extension.json) · [Nau, *Artificial Intelligence*, 1982](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~nau/papers/nau1982investigation.pdf)

The earlier self-play work trained compact adjustments to Tactical's evaluation. An early candidate lost its held-out comparison. A later 37-feature candidate completed only 13 of 32 planned held-out scenarios and was not promoted. The broader training audit recorded **157 goal rewards in 72,644 actions** spread across multiple runs. This demonstrates the sparsity of realized goals in those runs. A learned action ranker or a comparison of sampled and probability-weighted targets is a different experiment. Chess search distillation and Bot Bowl imitation provide precedent for using a policy as a teacher, with data-generation costs and teacher quality included in the comparison. Here the ladder provides direct reason to qualify a deeper teacher before trusting its labels. [Earlier held-out results](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/docs/selfplay-rl-2026-10-16/EVALUATION.md#held-out-result-and-release-decision) · [Later training audit](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/docs/selfplay-hour-2026-10-16/RESULTS.md) · [Ruoss et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/78f0db30c39c850de728c769f42fc903-Abstract-Conference.html)

## Pilot and decision to continue

**Primary question. Does using the known shot probabilities improve action selection most on positions where a strong finish requires several decisions?** Start with small tasks for the current shot and for buildup over a short, fixed horizon, then validate with paired games. Keep each method's legal actions and access to board information the same. Record computation, simulated transitions, and any teacher-label cost. Compare the quality of the chosen action at equal decision time and plot performance across transition budgets. Enumerating outcomes costs compute even when it needs fewer sampled games.

1. **Build the reference positions.** Combine eligible shots with naturally reached positions and short tactical exercises. Hold out entire position families or source games. Check each eligible keeper reply, verify the probability mass and branch consequences against the live engine, and look for shorter solutions before assigning a depth group. Use exact immediate targets. Use exact continuation labels only where a small verified state space permits them, and document a separate approximation or repeated-simulation reference elsewhere.
2. **Compare two chance treatments with the same ranker and search controls.** Use sampled outcomes in one arm and probability-weighted outcomes in the other. Measure error against the reference and the value lost by the chosen move, grouped by depth. Compare the immediate-goal keeper with a continuation-aware reply on the very same declarations. Keep training objectives aligned with the chosen match outcome. Treat game xG as a diagnostic or as a declared auxiliary-target ablation. Paying directly for every shot's xG could alter the incentive to keep possession or manage a lead. [Reward shaping and policy invariance](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/icml99-shaping.pdf)
3. **Check transfer and cost before scaling.** Use a whitelisted policy observation with legal actions and phase information. Withhold raw RNG state, seeds, and exact past rolls, since the engine stores some realized draws in action records. For disguised shots, use the public mixture when grading the keeper before target selection. Use multiple independent training runs and a frozen opponent pool, then report held-out match results, xG, tackles conceded, and decision time. Reproduce the generic sampled-versus-weighted comparison on backgammon or a small Bot Bowl scenario before claiming that the method generalizes. [Match random draw](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L460) · [Shot action record](https://github.com/yuanrui3/Football-chess/blob/45fc3f4a43f2775c1eaf6e27737ef4aba76a7403/dist/engine.js#L1332) · [RL evaluation and uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264)

The larger learning comparison can then use a scratch outcome learner, a Tactical imitation learner, and imitation followed by outcome-based self-play. Use the same action ranker and evaluation harness across these arms. That experiment would test whether the strengths of the environment make sparse outcome learning practical or whether the learnt policy needs a warm start. Known probability labels can be evaluated separately so their contribution is visible.

A reproducible release needs verified local targets and position groups that genuinely differ in the decisions needed to solve them. A larger learning study needs a practical performance or efficiency gain on held-out positions that also survives matched play. If the depth groups collapse under stronger replies or the learning advantage appears only on handpicked examples, publish that result and stop the benchmark effort at the pilot. The evidence available at the time supported running the pilot.
